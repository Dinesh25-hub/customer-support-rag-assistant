Damaged or Missing Items Policy (Version 1.1)

Page 1 of 1

1. Reporting a damaged item
If your item arrives damaged, torn, stained, or with a manufacturing fault, report it within 7 days of delivery. Include your order number and a clear photo of the problem.

2. Missing items
If your order arrives with an item missing, report it within 48 hours of delivery. We will check our packing record and ship the missing item at no cost within 3 working days.

3. Support tickets
Our support team reviews every damage report within 1 working day. Reports sent through the support chat are logged as tickets. You receive a ticket number by email.

4. Resolution
For confirmed damage, we offer a replacement or a full refund, including shipping. You do not need to return the damaged item.

5. Repeated issues
If the same product arrives damaged more than twice in a month, our quality team reviews the batch and may recall the stock.

Shipping Policy (Version 1.8)

1. Shipping options
Standard shipping takes 4 to 6 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and is available in metro cities only.

2. Shipping charges
Standard shipping is free on orders of ₹999 or more. Below ₹999, a flat fee of ₹79 applies. Express shipping costs ₹149 regardless of order value.

3. Order processing
Orders placed before 2 PM on a working day are processed the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM, or on weekends and public holidays, are processed the next working day.

4. Tracking
Once your order ships, you receive an email with a tracking link. Tracking updates may take up to 12 hours to appear after dispatch.

5. Delivery attempts
Our courier attempts delivery up to 3 times. After 3 failed attempts, the parcel returns to our warehouse and the order is cancelled with a full refund, minus a ₹149 re-shipping fee.

6. Incorrect address
We cannot change the delivery address after an order has shipped. If you entered the wrong address before shipping, contact support immediately.

7. Delayed orders
If your order is more than 3 days past the estimated delivery date, contact support. We will investigate with the courier and offer a refund or a replacement.

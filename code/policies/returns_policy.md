1. Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The return window starts on the day your order is marked as delivered, not the day you placed it.

2. Eligible items
Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have all original tags attached. Items must be in their original packaging where packaging was included.

3. Sale items
Sale items are final sale unless they arrive defective or damaged. Look for the label "Final Sale" on the product page. SKU TS-204 (Classic Oxford Shirt, Sale) is an example of a final sale item.

4. Defective or damaged items
If an item arrives defective or damaged, contact support within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund, including shipping costs. We do not require you to ship the damaged item back.

5. How to start a return
Log in to your account, open the order, and select "Return item." Print the prepaid label and drop the parcel at any partner courier location.

6. Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item. Your bank may take additional days to post the credit.

7. Items we cannot accept
We cannot accept returns for innerwear, swimwear, or items with broken seals on accessories. Gift cards are non-refundable.

8. Return shipping fee
Return shipping is free for defective items and for size exchanges. For other returns, a flat fee of ₹99 is deducted from the refund.

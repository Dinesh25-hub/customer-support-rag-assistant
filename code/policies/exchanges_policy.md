1. What you can exchange
You can exchange an item for a different size or colour of the same product within 30 days of delivery. Items must meet the same condition rules as returns.

2. How exchanges work
Start the exchange in your account under "Return or exchange." Choose the new size or colour. We ship the new item once the original is scanned at our warehouse, or within 2 days of your request if you choose the express exchange option.

3. Price differences
If the new item costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, we refund the difference to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

4. Out-of-stock exchanges
If the size you want is out of stock, we issue a full refund instead. You can then place a new order for the size you want.

5. Exchanges on sale items
Sale items cannot be exchanged for a different product. Size exchanges on sale items are allowed only if the same size is in stock and the item is not final sale.

6. Multiple exchanges
You can exchange an item only once per order. For a second exchange, contact support.
